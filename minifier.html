STARK EDITH DRONE

Flight Fiction Original — Inspired by Iron Man / MCU

The first complete Flight Fiction build. A 3D-printed quadcopter with custom-modelled armor shell and base frame — every part designed and printed from scratch. Shell styled after Stark Industries combat drone plating with angular fascia panels, a diamond vent, and a structural collar that bolts all four motor arms into one unified airframe.

◆ ABOUT THE BUILDER

Hi, I’m
Adithya.

I have a deep fascination for aircraft and a love for sci-fi and fantasy films. Growing up watching Iron Man and Captain America, I kept asking the same question: what would it actually take to build those?

Flight Fiction is the answer. Real builds — designed in CAD, 3D-printed, wired up, and flown. Every part you see in the gallery was modelled from scratch and built by hand. The goal is to keep scaling up until the aircraft are no longer micro-drones.
